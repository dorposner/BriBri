Pronunciation Guide

The Bribri language uses a Latin alphabet with special diacritics to indicate tones and unique sounds.

Vowels and Tones

  • High Tone (´): E.g., í. Pronounced with a higher pitch.
  • Low/Falling Tone (`): E.g., à. Pronounced with a lower or falling pitch.
  • Nasal Vowels (~): E.g., ã. Pronounced through the nose.

Consonants

  • Glottal Stop ('): E.g., Bua'. A brief pause or catch in the throat, similar to the middle of "uh-oh".
  • Sh / Ts: Common blends that are pronounced sharply.
Tip: Many words change their meaning based solely on their tone!
